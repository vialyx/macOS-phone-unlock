🔓 Welcome to PhoneUnlocker

Unlock your Mac's screensaver using Face ID on your iPhone with a single click. PhoneUnlocker uses Apple Pay's secure PassKit framework to bring biometric authentication to Mac screensaver control.

✨ Key Features

  • 🔐 Secure Face ID Authentication - Biometric verification
  • 📱 One-Click Unlock - From your iPhone
  • 💰 Zero-Dollar Transactions - No charges ever
  • ⚡ Instant Response - Sub-second unlock
  • 🎯 Menu Bar App - Clean, minimal interface

✅ System Requirements

  • macOS 13.0 or later (Ventura, Sonoma, or Sequoia)
  • Apple Pay configured on your Mac
  • iPhone with Face ID (iPhone XS or newer)
  • WiFi or Bluetooth connection between devices
  • Administrator access for installation

🚀 Installation Steps

  1. Click "Continue" to proceed with the installation
  2. Review the license agreement
  3. Select your installation location (usually /Applications)
  4. Enter your administrator password when prompted
  5. Wait for installation to complete
  6. Grant accessibility permissions when prompted
  7. Launch PhoneUnlocker from Applications

ℹ️ After Installation

After installation completes, you'll need to:

  • Grant Accessibility permissions (System Preferences → Security & Privacy → Accessibility)
  • Verify Apple Pay setup on your Mac and iPhone
  • Launch the application from your Applications folder
  • Test the unlock feature with your screensaver
